This tool applies Sergei Taneyev’s vertical shifting (invertible) counterpoint. Enter JV indices to see how intervals behave after inversion in 2- and 3-voice textures.

ℹ️ What is all this, I'm confused?

This tool was created for my own studies of Sergei Taneyev’s theories of invertible counterpoint, inspired by Jacob Gran’s video lectures on the subject (YouTube playlist).

It is meant as a way to explore and visualize interval transformations after inversion, both for my own practice and as a resource for anyone else who may find it useful.
